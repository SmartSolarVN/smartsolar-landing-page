Renewable energy for everyone

Electricity prices are rising across Southeast Asia.
In Vietnam alone, prices have increased 4x in the last two years.

Small and medium-sized businesses feel the impact on their bottom line the most.
At the same time, the region has some of the world’s strongest solar potential.

Solar energy is the obvious answer, but for many, it remains out of reach due to high upfront investment costs.
We are changing that.

Through our solar subscription model, homes and businesses can power their operations with the sun, with zero upfront cost and instant savings from day one.
